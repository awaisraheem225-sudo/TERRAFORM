SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Slope uses finite differences on a spherical reference geometry.
2. East-west pixel distance varies with latitude.
3. The cylindrical grid becomes singular at the poles; cells at and beyond the polar cutoff are excluded.
4. Roughness and local relief use angular 3x3 grid-cell neighbourhoods, not constant physical-distance windows.
5. The global MOLA product includes interpolated elevation values in zero-observation cells.
6. Observed-only statistics select centre cells with one or more recorded observations.
7. Neighbourhood calculations may still include interpolated neighbouring elevations, even when the centre cell has a recorded observation.
8. The observation mask identifies cells with recorded observations, not the quality or accuracy of each individual elevation.
9. Area-weighted means use spherical reference cell areas.
10. Weighted percentiles are approximate histogram-bin estimates.
11. These metrics do not independently validate the source elevation data or interpolation.
